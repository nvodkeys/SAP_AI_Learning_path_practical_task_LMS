# SAP SE Presale Research Brief: Order-to-Cash, Support Operations and Cloud Delivery (Kickoff Prep for EPAM)

The most credible entry point is not "SAP needs SAP software." SAP's evidence-backed pressure points sit in three places: turning a record €24.1 billion current cloud backlog into revenue (the backlog is growing faster than revenue and skews toward back-end-loaded mega-deals); cloud gross margin, which SAP's Q3 2026 Quarterly Statement shows slipped 0.8 points year on year to 74.3% (–0.7pp at constant currencies) as cost of cloud grew faster than cloud revenue; and a customer-trust deficit (Customer NPS 9 vs. a 12–16 target, binding EU commitments on support practices, and the ECC 2027 cliff). These are best addressed with SI-delivered "SAP runs SAP" acceleration: BTP extensions, data/AI, migration factories, test automation and support operations, not net-new licenses.

## TL;DR
- **Core pains:** (1) backlog-to-revenue conversion and RISE migration throughput; (2) cloud gross margin compression from AI, hardware and sovereign-cloud build-out; (3) customer dissatisfaction and contract friction (NPS 9 vs. 12–16 target, uncapped renewal hikes, EU-mandated support flexibility); plus a lingering US public-sector pricing probe.
- **Why now:** ECC mainstream maintenance ends 31 Dec 2027, and Gartner projects about 17,000 holdouts, "nearly half" of the 35,000-customer ECC base, still unmigrated by 2027 (CIO.com, June 2025). The EU commitments became binding on 9 July 2026 for 10 years, globally, monitored by an independent trustee. Cloud CSAT replaced NPS as an Executive Board bonus KPI from 2026.\[1\] SAP guides to accelerating revenue in 2027 and has to deliver it.\[2\]\[3\]
- **EPAM play:** go in with a paid 6–8 week assessment plus pilot on (a) a RISE "migration factory" accelerator for SAP's own partner-led migrations (test automation, custom-code remediation, AI-assisted conversion), (b) a BDC/Datasphere backlog-to-revenue and renewal-risk cockpit, and (c) agentic support triage on BTP alongside Joule in SAP for Me. In every case, frame EPAM as an extension of SAP's delivery capacity, not as an advisor telling SAP how to run SAP.

## A) Executive Brief (1 page)

**Top 5 pain-point hypotheses**

1. **Backlog-to-revenue conversion lag (O2C / RISE delivery).** CCB grew 26% at constant currencies to €24.1B in Q3 2026.\[4\]\[5\] Large deals are "back-end-loaded," so mission-critical ERP often doesn't go live in year one.\[6\] Hypothesis: migration and implementation throughput, much of it partner-delivered, is the binding constraint on recognizing revenue. Pressure peaks in 2027, when SAP has promised revenue acceleration.
2. **Cloud gross margin erosion (Cloud Ops).** Non-IFRS cloud gross margin was 74.3% in Q3 2026, down from 75.1%.\[7\] Cost of cloud rose 29% vs. 26% cloud revenue growth. Management attributes this to AI investment and higher hardware costs, while committing more than €20B over 10 years to sovereign cloud.\[8\]\[9\] At roughly €26B annual cloud revenue, each 1pp of cloud gross margin is worth about €260M of gross profit.
3. **Customer trust and support experience (Support / CX).** Customer NPS fell to 9 in 2025 against a 12–16 target.\[1\] A Gartner report quoted by The Register (May 2025) found that tasks such as enabling a new interface to a satellite system were "taking over a month to complete," with no committed resolution time for service requests, and Gartner's Alessandro Galimberti put the standard uptime SLA at 99.7%, below the 99.9% industry standard. From 2026 the Board is paid on Cloud CSAT, so this is now a compensation-linked KPI.\[1\]
4. **Commercial / contract friction and compliance exposure (Q2C governance).** EU binding commitments (July 2026) force landscape splitting, termination rights, removal of reinstatement fees and single-metric licensing for on-prem support.\[10\]\[11\] All of this has to be operationalized in contract, billing and entitlement systems under an independent trustee.\[12\] Gartner flags uncapped 10%+ private-cloud renewal increases.\[13\] The US DOJ civil probe into SAP/Carahsoft public-sector pricing (ongoing since at least 2022) adds partner-channel pricing-governance risk.\[14\]
5. **Legacy-to-subscription revenue transition (O2C mix).** Software support revenue fell 8% in the first nine months of 2026, and SAP expects the decline rate "to accelerate."\[4\]\[15\] Every support-to-cloud conversion has to be priced, credited, contracted, billed and recognized correctly. That creates a high-volume, error-prone conversion process where leakage and disputes are likely (hypothesis).

**Why these matter now**
- The ECC 2027 deadline, together with SAP's 2027 revenue-acceleration promise, puts delivery capacity at the center of the equity story.
- Q4 typically carries about half of annual bookings, so any friction in quote-to-contract surfaces immediately.\[16\]
- AI and M&A (Reltio, Dremio, Prior Labs) are already diluting margin.\[17\] Operating-efficiency stories are welcome internally.\[18\]\[19\]

**Likely board-level KPI impact**
- CCB growth (30% weight in the 2025 STI) and cloud revenue growth.\[20\]
- Cloud gross margin and non-IFRS operating profit (€11.8–12.2B 2026 outlook).\[21\]\[22\]
- Free cash flow (SAP's Q3 2026 statement guides to approximately €10 billion at actual currencies, vs. €8.24 billion in 2025).
- Cloud CSAT (new 2026 STI KPI), replacing NPS.\[1\]
- Regulatory compliance: EU trustee reporting. Penalties of up to 10% of turnover are possible without a new infringement finding.\[12\]

## C) Kickoff Preparation Pack

### 1. Assumptions to bring proactively (flagged as hypotheses)
1. SAP's internal core (finance, contract-to-revenue, order-to-cash) runs on SAP Business Suite / Cloud ERP via RISE. SAP said in Oct 2025 it "has moved from SAP ECC to SAP Business Suite and SAP Cloud ERP, through RISE with SAP."\[47\] The exact edition and go-live date are not public.
2. Lead-to-cash starts in SAP CX (cloud) and hands off to S/4HANA for invoicing and core finance (documented in 2020).\[25\] The internal subscription billing / RAR stack is assumed SAP-native but **not publicly verified**.
3. The binding constraint on 2027 revenue acceleration is delivery throughput (ramps and go-lives), not demand.
4. Cloud GM pressure is partly structural (AI/GPU, sovereign) and partly operational (landscape sprawl, provisioning inefficiency). Only the latter is addressable by an SI.
5. Support-to-cloud conversion credits and multi-generation packaging create reconciliation and dispute workload in billing and revenue accounting.
6. EU commitments require new contract/entitlement logic and trustee-grade audit evidence. Some of it is likely still manual four months after the decision.
7. Support case handling is being re-platformed around Joule in SAP for Me and agentic case resolution. Adoption and accuracy metrics are still early.\[48\]
8. Cloud CSAT measurement (new for 2026) needs a data foundation linking contract, landscape, incident and survey data.
9. SAP is "customer zero" for BDC and Joule Work (110,000 employees, claimed 20% productivity gains in finance, HR and procurement).\[47\]\[49\] EPAM should position as an accelerator of that agenda, not a challenger to it.
10. SAP's partner ecosystem, which EPAM is part of, is a lever SAP actively manages. EPAM's value is incremental capacity plus engineering depth (AI, test automation, data), not functional SAP consulting alone.

### 2. Discovery questions by category
- **Business:** Which customer segments have the largest backlog-to-go-live gap? What share of 2027 cloud revenue depends on ramps not yet live? How is Cloud CSAT defined and baselined?
- **Process:** Where do support-to-subscription conversions break (pricing, credit, contract, billing, RAR)? What is the cycle time from RISE contract signature to first productive tenant? How are EU-commitment requests (landscape split, termination) processed today?
- **IT architecture:** Which internal systems handle quote, contract, provisioning, billing, revenue recognition and case management? What runs on BTP vs. S/4 core? How much third-party tooling (e.g., Salesforce, ServiceNow, Microsoft) remains in GTM/support? This is **unverified**, so ask.
- **Data:** Is there a single contract-to-consumption data product in BDC? Can cost of cloud be attributed per customer tenant? How is the incident-to-renewal linkage modeled?
- **Governance:** Who owns the trustee reporting under the EU commitments? How are AI-agent decisions in support audited? What partner-delivery quality KPIs exist for RISE?

### 3. "Day-1 value" quick wins (30–90 days)
- **Backlog conversion heatmap:** BDC/Datasphere data product joining CCB contracts, provisioning status, project milestones (Cloud ALM) and consumption, to flag stalled ramps. Output: top 50 at-risk ramps with recommended actions.
- **Agentic case triage pilot:** a BTP AI Core extension to SAP for Me / agentic case resolution for one product line (e.g., RISE service requests), with measured deflection, routing accuracy and time-to-resolution.
- **Support-to-cloud conversion reconciliation bot:** automated checks across CPQ, contract, BRIM/billing and RAR for a sample of conversions, to quantify leakage and dispute drivers (this produces the missing baseline).
- **Migration test-automation kit:** reusable regression packs for common RISE conversion scenarios, offered for SAP-led customer migrations to shorten test cycles.
- **Signavio process mining on internal quote-to-cash:** baseline cycle times, rework loops and discount-approval conformance.

### 4. Mid-term transformation themes (6–18 months)
- **Migration factory as a service** for SAP's RISE pipeline: AI-assisted custom-code remediation, data migration, automated testing, cutover rehearsal.
- **Cloud FinOps platform:** cost-per-tenant transparency, automated landscape right-sizing, GPU/AI inference cost governance.
- **Autonomous support operations:** Service Cloud v2 + Joule agents + Cloud ALM proactive monitoring + ECS/hyperscaler ticket orchestration via Integration Suite.
- **Compliance-by-design contract platform** for EU commitments: segment-level support contracts, entitlement engine, trustee audit data products.
- **Renewal intelligence:** CSAT, incident, consumption and contract signals combined into renewal-risk scoring and next-best-action for customer success.

### 5. Risks, dependencies and change management
- **Political sensitivity:** advising SAP on SAP. Frame as capacity and engineering acceleration, use SAP's own vocabulary ("Autonomous Enterprise," "customer zero," "clean core") and avoid implying product gaps.
- **Partner competition:** large SIs (Accenture, Deloitte, Capgemini) hold incumbent positions. EPAM should differentiate on engineering, AI and test automation.
- **Data access:** internal financial and contract data are highly restricted (MNPI before earnings). Expect clean-room or synthetic data for pilots.
- **Organizational churn:** restructuring of about 10,000 positions in 2024 (€3.2B expense)\[50\] and ongoing AI-driven reorganization may leave process-ownership gaps. Confirm accountable owners early.
- **Legal sensitivity:** DOJ probe and EU trustee. Avoid scope that touches pricing evidence unless Legal sponsors it.

## F) Facts vs. Hypotheses

**Facts (sourced)**
- Q3 2026: CCB €24.144B (+28%, +26% cc); cloud revenue €6.668B (+26%); non-IFRS cloud GM 74.3% vs 75.1%; software support revenue €2.434B (−5%); total revenue €10.27B.\[4\]\[7\]\[59\] Cost of cloud +29%.
- 2026 outlook: cloud revenue toward the upper end of €25.8–26.2B; non-IFRS operating profit €11.8–12.2B (cut ~€0.1B in Q2 for Dremio/Prior Labs dilution); FCF about €10B; CCB growth to slightly decelerate; total revenue growth to accelerate in 2027; support revenue decline to accelerate.\[2\]\[3\]\[4\]\[21\]\[22\]\[60\]
- FY2025: cloud revenue €21.02B;\[61\] total cloud backlog €77B (+30%); Customer NPS 9 vs 12–16 target; NPS replaced by Cloud CSAT from 2026 in Board compensation.\[1\]\[23\]
- 2024 transformation program: about 10,000 positions affected; about €3.2B expenses; €2.5B paid out in 2024.\[50\]
- EU: probe opened Sept 2025; commitments binding 9 July 2026; per the Commission decision, they apply globally for 10 years and are monitored by an independent monitoring trustee; on-prem support only.
- DOJ: civil investigation into SAP/Carahsoft public-sector pricing since at least 2022; SAP cooperating; no findings published.\[14\]
- Sovereign cloud: more than €20B over 10 years (announced 2 Sept 2025).\[46\]
- Joule in SAP for Me and agentic case resolution: rollout from May 2026, no extra cost.\[29\]\[62\]
- Internal: SAP converted its corporate ERP from ECC 6.0 to S/4HANA in one go (2020, CIO Florian Roth), cutting modifications from about 3,000 to about 1,500. In 2025 SAP stated it now runs SAP Business Suite / Cloud ERP via RISE and is customer zero for BDC. Joule Work is live with 110,000 employees (SAP claim).\[25\]\[47\]\[49\]
- Gartner: 39% of about 35,000 ECC customers migrated by end-2024; about 17,000 holdouts projected at end-2027.\[40\]\[63\]

**Hypotheses (inferred; validate)**
- Delivery throughput, not demand, constrains backlog conversion.
- Support-to-cloud conversion creates measurable billing and revenue-recognition rework or leakage (no public baseline exists).
- Part of the cloud GM decline is operationally addressable (landscape sprawl, provisioning).
- EU commitment processes are partly manual.
- SAP's internal billing / RAR / support case stack is SAP-native (not publicly verified; third-party tools may persist).
- EPAM quantitative ranges in this brief are reasoned estimates, not observed SAP data.

## Caveats
- SAP's internal billing, revenue-recognition and support case systems are not publicly documented beyond the 2020 CIO article and 2025–2026 keynotes. Treat all internal-architecture statements as assumptions to validate.
- Quantitative value ranges are reasoned estimates (e.g., 1pp cloud GM ≈ €260M on about €26B cloud revenue). They are not SAP-disclosed figures.
- Several customer-sentiment data points come from secondary or trade media (e3mag, The Register, aggregator blogs). The DSAG 2025 sample was small (243) and SAP-distributed.\[55\]
- The DOJ matter is an unproven civil investigation. Do not raise it directly in the kickoff; use it only to inform pricing-governance positioning.
